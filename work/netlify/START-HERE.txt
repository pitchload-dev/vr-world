KIT VENTURE HALL — STATIC PREVIEW

For investment-interest and follow-up storage and the protected tables, use the separate
kit-venture-hall-netlify-backend.zip package and its START-HERE.md.
Netlify Drop alone does not deploy the backend function. Saving will show an
error until the function is deployed; it never shows a fake success.

UPLOAD
1. Unzip kit-venture-hall-netlify.zip.
2. Open https://app.netlify.com/drop and sign in to your Netlify account.
3. Drag the extracted kit-venture-hall-netlify folder into the upload area.
   The folder you upload must contain index.html directly inside it.
4. Wait for deployment, then open the HTTPS address Netlify gives you.

No build command, terminal, installation, API key or server is needed.
The JavaScript, styles, font, saved Pitchload data and preview video are bundled.
Keep _headers and _redirects with index.html: they enable the headset permissions
and the meeting page routing on Netlify.

META QUEST 3
Open the Netlify HTTPS address directly in Meta Quest Browser.
Select Enter VR and allow the browser's immersive VR permission.
Use your controllers: point at the floor and press the trigger to teleport.
Use the LEFT thumbstick to walk in the direction you push it, relative to your
headset: forward is forward, right is sideways right, and likewise for back/left.
Push gently to move slowly; push fully for walking speed. Looking up or down does
not move you vertically. Booth walls, furniture and hall boundaries stop movement.
Move the right thumbstick sideways to snap turn.
Center the sticks after closing a reader or the headset menu to resume movement.
The green floor ring marks a valid teleport spot; a coral ring marks a blocked one.
Controller rays and selection dots highlight interactive objects.
Use PREV BOOTH / NEXT BOOTH on your left controller to explore in sequence.
Select a presentation screen to open a paginated reader in front of you.
Squeeze either controller to bring an open reader back into view.
Point and trigger-select slide arrows, profile pages and careers cards.
The controller menu returns to the information desk or exits VR.
Select the careers shelf to bring a readable job card in front of you.
Use Read more and Next role when available, then Close to return to the hall.
The Request follow up kiosk opens the contact form without leaving VR.
Choose an example event day and time, confirm the preview, and return to the booth.
The floating panels stay in place while you look around. Movement is paused until
you close them, so you can read and select comfortably.
Select Unverbindliches Interesse bekunden on an investment banner to open the
German interest preview. Choose Yes or No; with Yes, choose an optional amount.
In VR, use the amount presets and plus/minus controls. On desktop, type an amount.
Interesse speichern sends the selected answer to the backend. A confirmation
appears only after successful storage. Cancel does not submit the form.

DESKTOP
Choose Enter the exhibition. Drag the scene to look around.
Use W A S D or the arrow keys to walk.
Choose a booth on the floor plan, or use the startup directory.
Click company screens, profile boards, slide arrows and careers cards.
The Video tab in the company popup contains the preview film.
Hover over objects to see their outline and an action hint. Click a 3D exhibit
to activate its concept; select it again to reset. Each plinth names its action.
Use the nearby-booth shortcuts or the Next booth button.
Visited booths and your current booth are marked on the map.

WHAT IS INCLUDED
- Subtle shimmering outlines on controls, hover highlights and brief click feedback.
  Nearby 3D objects shimmer one at a time; VR buttons and keyboard keys respond
  without moving the reading surface or changing controller targets.
  Reduced-motion preferences and Pause exhibit animation disable these effects.
- New simple 3D concepts, each with a specific interaction. Read EXHIBITS.md for
  the meaning and sources. Sparseon remains a labeled neutral concept until its
  activity is confirmed by the event organizer.
- Redesigned booths with a large overview board: company summary, founding year,
  team size and listed job count. Missing facts appear as a dash.
- A profile board replaces the video wall: Our Product, Our Company, Headquarters.
  Select it to read every supplied profile field, with page controls in VR.
- Video is available only in the company popup.
- An inward-facing investment panel.
- Career shelves show individual API roles; selecting a card opens that exact role.
- The information desk shows startup names only.
- The third top navigation tab opens the password-protected interest table.
- Follow-up Requests opens a separate table using the same protected login.
- VR investment amount increments: 10, 100, 500, 1,000 or 10,000 euros.
- Eleven named startup booths and one information desk.
- Illuminated booth frames, central 3D concept exhibits and vertical careers racks.
- Interactive preview slides and an 18-second silent preview film.
- Published sample job listings where available.
- Meeting requests from desktop and VR are saved when the backend is deployed.
  No day or time selection. Name/email are optional in both browser and VR.
  A controller-operated keyboard enables VR contact entry. No invitations are sent.
- Close-up careers cards and a meeting-request popup for desktop and VR.
- A seeking-investment banner on every booth, with clearly labeled sample amounts.
- A German non-binding interest dialog inspired by the supplied ONINO screenshot.
  Responses are stored for the event team when the backend is deployed.
  Nothing is submitted to ONINO or Pitchload. Investment amounts remain examples.

Company-specific decks, videos,
